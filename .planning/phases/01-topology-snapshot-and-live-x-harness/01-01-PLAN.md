<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| XRandR reply facts → detached topology model | Untrusted server-controlled counts, dimensions, Atom names, and output arrays become owned core state. |
| Recoverable allocator → publication | Resource failure must not leak, partially publish, or destroy the last committed object. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-01 | Tampering / Denial of Service | `monitor_topology_snapshot_create()` | high | mitigate | Checked arithmetic, complete deep copy, structural validation, and exhaustive malformed-count/allocation-failure native tests before publication. |
| T-01-02 | Tampering | monitor Atom names | medium | mitigate | Reject unresolved, duplicate, empty, or invalid-UTF-8 names before they enter the committed snapshot; retain numeric Atom identity. |
| T-01-03 | Denial of Service | snapshot ownership | high | mitigate | Recoverable allocator seam, single ownership transfer, epoch-overflow refusal, NULL-safe destruction, and fail-index tests preserve last-good state. |
</threat_model>
